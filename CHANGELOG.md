# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- Release packaging only: CurseForge uploads now match game versions by patch name and type, so every client's zip is published. No addon changes.

## 0.1.0

First release, for Retail, Forever, Classic Era, Burning Crusade Anniversary and Mists of Pandaria Classic.

- Record every enemy player you slay and every enemy player who slays you, account-wide, with per-character, world, battleground and arena counts.
- Open-book Tome with race and class pages that unlock as you slay foes, class icons and a movable frame.
- Milestone, overall and special titles, with an epithet you can display in the Tome.
- Unlock toasts, nemesis alerts after repeated deaths to the same foe, and built-in WoW sounds.
- Recent encounters ledger and per-nemesis history pages.
- Settings inside the Tome and in the game's AddOns settings, plus a minimap launcher.
- On Midnight-engine clients (Retail 12.x, Forever), where addons cannot read the combat log, kills come from engaged enemies dying and deaths from Blizzard's death recap.
